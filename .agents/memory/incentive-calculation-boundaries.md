---
name: Incentive calculation boundaries
description: Why calculators deliberately avoid generic subsidy deductions and how to assess future funding claims.
---

Show gross investment estimates and refer to the official Pronovo calculation rather than restoring a convenient fixed percentage or CHF/kWp subsidy formula.

**Why:** The authoritative incentive audit exposed contradictions between generic central figures, blog examples and canton claims. A defensible calculation requires commissioning date, installation type, capacity, eligibility and applicable bonuses. Removing the incomplete calculation was intentional, not a missing feature to fill with an estimate.

**How to apply:** Only introduce automatic subsidy deductions with a complete, dated tariff model and eligibility tests. Do not substitute zero when the amount is unknown. Confirm whether local maximums include federal aid before adding contributions. An announcement or proposal is not evidence of an enacted utility tariff; keep uncertain rates explicitly qualified.
