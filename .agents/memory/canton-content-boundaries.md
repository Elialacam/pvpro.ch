---
name: Canton content boundaries
description: Why new canton translations deliberately omit legacy local claims and financial sections.
---
Expanded canton coverage must use neutral project guidance unless local facts are verified. Do not fill omitted sections with another Canton's incentives, statistics, testimonials, installer coverage, or financial assumptions.

**Why:** The structural expansion was explicitly authorized without changing existing canton content, titles or descriptions, and without inventing local claims. Legacy pages and newly added pages therefore intentionally differ in section coverage.

**How to apply:** Preserve that distinction when maintaining the shared template. Adding geographic or language coverage does not authorize copying legacy financial or local claims into new pages.

For editorial simplification of the established guides, preserve verified facts and their qualifications; prefer plain explanations over cutting content to meet a word count.

**Why:** The user confirmed the factual foundation and requested easier reading for homeowners, not shorter commercial landing pages or another research pass.

**How to apply:** Put the practical answer before the official term. Keep eligibility exceptions, dates, source attribution and FAQ coverage when moving details out of a hero or table. A design brief's highlighted facts are not exhaustive: retain useful verified details from its accompanying research dossier too. Limit changes to the explicitly requested languages and Cantons.
