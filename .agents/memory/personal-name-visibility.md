name: Personal name visibility
description: Where the founder's personal name may appear on the public site.
---
The user permits their personal name only in team content and Datenschutz/privacy pages. Remove it from other public pages, including Impressum, blog bylines, author biographies, metadata, structured data and machine-readable site summaries.

**Why:** The user objected to unrelated pages appearing in Google searches for their personal name.

**How to apply:** Attribute blog material to PvPro.ch rather than the founder. Do not restore personal-name references from old company documents outside the allowed contexts. Changes to site content do not instantly remove cached search snippets or external-site mentions.
